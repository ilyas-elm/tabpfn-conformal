TabPFN-3.5 · Bank Account Fraud

What can you actually promise a regulator?

A fraud model gives you a probability. A risk committee needs a guarantee. Conformal prediction converts one into the other — but only as far as your confirmed fraud labels allow. Every figure below is computed live from real TabPFN-3.5 predictions.

01 · The ceiling

Your label budget sets a hard limit

A calibration set of size n can only certify α ≥ 1/(n+1) — the threshold is the ⌈(n+1)(1−α)⌉-th smallest score, and that index cannot exceed n. Split conformal spends half your positives on calibration. Cross-conformal spends none.

The same guarantee from half the labels

400 real transactions, routed live
